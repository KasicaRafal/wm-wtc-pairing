Privacy Policy

Overview

WTC Pairing Planner (“the App”) is a free, unofficial fan-made web tool for Warmachine World Team Championship 2026 pairings. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

Short version: the App does not collect accounts, ratings, or personal data. Matchup scores and pairing math stay in your browser. The page is a static site: your browser loads the App files, and any ratings you enter are stored locally unless you export a file yourself.

Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not create accounts and does not collect, store on our servers, or sell personal information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or account identifiers
  • Device identifiers (such as advertising IDs or hardware IDs)
  • Location data
  • Usage, behavioral, or advertising analytics
  • Crash reports or diagnostics sent to us
  • Your matchup ratings, list choices, or pairing results

How the App Works

When you open the App, your browser loads the static page, styles, scripts, and a public team/list data file from the same website. Rating, export, import, and pairing calculations then run entirely on your device.

Ratings you enter are saved in your browser’s local storage so they persist if you refresh the page. Clearing site data for this website removes them. JSON and CSV exports are files you choose to download or share; we never receive a copy unless you send one yourself.

There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking pixels in the App.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.

The pages load web fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com). Your browser may send a standard request to Google to fetch those fonts. That request is handled by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy, not ours. We do not receive that data as identifiable visitor information.

The optional Ko-fi support link goes to ko-fi.com/kasi_r. If you follow it, Ko-fi’s own terms and privacy policy apply. Clicking the link is never required to use the App.

A link to the WTC 2026 listing on Longshanks (warmachine.longshanks.org/event/37180) is provided so you can check official lists. Following that link is optional; Longshanks’ own terms and privacy policy then apply.

This Website’s Hosting (GitHub Pages)

The App does not collect your ratings. This website, however, is hosted on GitHub Pages, a third-party service operated by GitHub, Inc. When you visit any page here, GitHub automatically processes certain technical data about your visit.

Specifically, when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor’s IP address is logged and stored by GitHub for security purposes, regardless of whether the visitor is signed in to GitHub. This processing is performed by GitHub to maintain the security and integrity of its service; it is not used by us for tracking, analytics, or marketing, and we do not have access to it as identifiable visitor data.

This processing is governed by GitHub’s own policies, not ours. For details, see the GitHub Privacy Statement and GitHub Pages data collection.

Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Because we do not collect user accounts or ratings, the App is intended to be safe for all age groups from a data-privacy standpoint.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach the developer through Ko-fi.

This policy covers the WTC Pairing Planner website. It does not cover third-party websites or services that may be linked from the App, including GitHub, Google Fonts, Ko-fi, and Longshanks.
