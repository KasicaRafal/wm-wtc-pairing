WTC Pairing Planner 2026 is a free fan-made tool for Warmachine World Team Championship pairings.

Rate your matchups, export JSON for your teammates, import all five files, and compute 5v5 pairings — maximize total score or avoid the worst tables. Pairing reports stay in your browser.

Team and list data is a snapshot from Longshanks event 37180 for WTC 2026. Names and lists may be incomplete, outdated, or mis-parsed — check the event page for the official lists.

Ratings and pairing math run entirely in your browser. We do not collect your scores. Exports are files you choose to share. Privacy Policy
